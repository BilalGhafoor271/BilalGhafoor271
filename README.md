### Research & Manuscripts

- **Zero-Shot Anomaly Detection with Multimodal LLMs**  
  Reliability-driven visual anomaly detection framework combining vision-language models and MC-Dropout uncertainty estimation on MVTec AD (**97.7% AUROC**, **90.3% accuracy**).  
  [Code](https://github.com/BilalGhafoor271/Zero_shot_anomoly_detection_research_paper_code)

- **GEO Satellite Detection & Restoration in Astronomical Imagery** *(In Preparation)*  
  Custom spatial filtering and localized median inpainting pipeline across **3,768 FITS images**, isolating stationary GEO satellite signatures and resolving dual-satellite cases (~71 px apart) with zero alteration to clean background pixels.

- **Multi-Class Dental Disease Classification from Panoramic Radiographs**  
  Hybrid EfficientNet-B4 and ResNet50 architecture with CBAM attention and Grad-CAM explainability across 11 dental conditions (**85.67% Micro F1**).  
  [Code](https://github.com/BilalGhafoor271/Multi-Class-Dental-Disease-Classification-from-Panoramic-Radiographs)

- **Heart MRI Myocardial Segmentation**  
  3D deep learning pipeline for cardiac MRI segmentation (LV/RV walls, core, and border-zone surfaces).  
  [Code](https://github.com/BilalGhafoor271/Heart-MRI-Myocardial-Segmentation-TFM-)
