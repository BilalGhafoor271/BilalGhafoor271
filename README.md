## Selected Research & Technical Work

### Research & Multimodal / Vision Systems
- **Zero-Shot Anomaly Detection (MVTec AD / MLLMs)**  
  Reliability-driven multimodal anomaly detection with uncertainty estimation, achieving **97.7% AUROC** and **90.3% accuracy**[cite: 1].
- **GEO Satellite Detection & Restoration (Astronomical FITS)**  
  Custom spatial filtering and localized median inpainting across **3,768 FITS images**—preserving 100% of clean pixels while isolating stationary GEO satellite signatures and resolving dual-satellite cases (~71 px apart)[cite: 1].
- **Medical & 3D Vision Pipelines**  
  Multi-class dental radiograph classification (**85.67% Micro F1**, 11 conditions with CBAM & Grad-CAM), cardiac MRI myocardial segmentation, and multi-view 3D plant reconstruction (**Open3D** / **Three.js**)[cite: 1].

### Applied AI Solutions & Production Systems
- **Society Gate Control & License Plate Recognition (ALPR)**  
  Real-time vehicle/plate detection and OCR pipeline (**YOLO** / **OpenCV** / **PaddleOCR**) paired with automated entry/exit logging and reporting[cite: 1, 2].
- **AI-First Web, CRM & RAG Workflows**  
  Production AI web platforms, CRM integrations, and document-grounded assistants (**Blueprint Assistant**) built with **FastAPI**, **LangChain**, **React**, **TypeScript**, and **Supabase**[cite: 1, 2].
- **Edge AI, Robotics & Sports Motion Systems**  
  Sensor-fusion and **ESP32** hardware control systems, vision-guided robotics, and **CoachAI** pose/motion analysis pipelines[cite: 1, 2].
